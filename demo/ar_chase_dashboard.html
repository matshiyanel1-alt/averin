DATA PROCESSING AGREEMENT
Responsible Party: [Client Firm Name], a company registered in the Republic of South Africa ("Responsible Party").
Operator: LMNR Group (Pty) Ltd, trading as Averin, registration number 2026/828138/07, registered in the Republic of South Africa ("Operator").
1. Purpose & Scope
The Operator processes personal information on behalf of the Responsible Party solely for the purpose of automated accounts receivable collections, as described in the service agreement. Processing is limited to: debtor name, contact number, email address, invoice reference, and outstanding amount.
2. Data Residency
All personal information is processed and stored within the Republic of South Africa (AWS Cape Town region, af-south-1). No personal information is transferred outside South Africa without prior written consent.
3. Operator Obligations
The Operator shall: (a) process personal information only on documented instruction; (b) implement appropriate technical and organisational measures; (c) notify the Responsible Party of a data breach within 72 hours; (d) assist with data subject rights requests within 30 days; (e) delete or return all personal information upon termination.
4. Retention & Deletion
Audit logs are retained for 7 years for legal defensibility. Call recordings are retained for 30 days. All other account data is deleted within 90 days of subscription termination. A full data export is provided on request before deletion.
5. Sub-Processors
Authorised sub-processors: Bland AI (voice calls, US infrastructure — covered under a separate SCCs addendum); AWS Cape Town (hosting); SendGrid (email delivery).
